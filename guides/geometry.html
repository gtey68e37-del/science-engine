Circle & Triangle Formulas • 3D Sphere Surface & Volume • Degrees to Radians & Trig Ratios

More Information

The Geometry & Trigonometry tool in Apex Science Engine computes exact measurements for 2D shapes, 3D solids, and trigonometric angles. For a Circle with radius r, it calculates Area (A = πr²) and Circumference (C = 2πr). For a Triangle with base b and height h, it computes Area (A = ½ × b × h) along with the right-triangle hypotenuse c = √(b² + h²).

Circle: A = πr², C = 2πr  |  Sphere: S = 4πr², V = (4/3)πr³  |  Radians: rad = θ × (π ÷ 180)

In 3D Sphere mode, entering radius r gives both Surface Area (S = 4πr², measured in square units) and Volume (V = (4/3)πr³, measured in cubic units). Notice how radius r is squared for surface area and cubed for volume, meaning doubling the radius multiplies surface area by 4 and volume by 8.

In Trigonometry mode, the tool converts an angle θ from degrees into radians using rad = θ × (π ÷ 180) before evaluating sin(θ), cos(θ), and tan(θ). For example, at θ = 30°, rad ≈ 0.5236, sin(30°) = 0.5000, cos(30°) ≈ 0.8660, and tan(30°) ≈ 0.5774. At 90° or 270°, cos(θ) = 0, making tan(θ) = sin(θ) ÷ cos(θ) undefined.

Common mistakes in geometry and trigonometry include confusing diameter with radius (r = diameter ÷ 2), forgetting the ½ factor in triangle area, or plugging degrees directly into radian-based formulas without multiplying by π ÷ 180 first.
