Statistics Tool Guide: Mean, Extremes & Median

Count & Sum • Mean (Average = Sum / Count) • Extremes (Max & Min) • Median

More Information

The Statistics tool in Apex Science Engine analyzes numerical datasets entered as comma-separated values, such as 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. First, it computes Count (how many numbers are in the list) and Sum (the total of all numbers added together). For 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, Count = 5 and Sum = 150.00.[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)

Mean = Sum ÷ Count  |  Extremes: Max (Largest) & Min (Smallest)  |  Median: Middle Value After Sorting

Next, the tool finds the Mean (Average) by dividing Sum by Count: 150.00 ÷ 5 = 30.0000. It also reports Extremes: the Maximum (Max, largest value, here 50) and Minimum (Min, smallest value, here 10), which define the outer boundaries of the data.[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)

Another essential measure in statistics is the Median (the middle value). To find the median correctly, you must first sort the numbers in order—either from smallest to largest (ascending) or from largest to smallest (descending). For example, in 12, 4, 19, 8, 15, sorting from smallest to largest gives 4, 8, 12, 15, 19, and the middle number is 12. Sorting from largest to smallest (19, 15, 12, 8, 4) gives the exact same middle value: 12. If there is an even count of numbers, the median is the average of the two middle numbers.

Watch out for common mistakes when analyzing data: forgetting to sort numbers before picking the middle value for the median, confusing the largest number (Max) with the mean, or dividing by the wrong count when values are missing. Always double-check how many numbers are in your dataset before calculating.
